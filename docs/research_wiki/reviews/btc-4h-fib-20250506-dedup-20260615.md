## Visual / structural conclusion

data: 05-06 08:00 low = 93988, **05-06 12:00 low = 93663 (the actual swing bottom)**, then

correct and A a redundant, slightly worse version. Moving A's anchor_a to 93663 would make
it an exact duplicate of B, so the correct action is **dedup/supersede**, not correction.
