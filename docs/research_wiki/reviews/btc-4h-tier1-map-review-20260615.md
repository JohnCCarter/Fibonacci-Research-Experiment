## Threshold Rule

**Lokal densitet per zon avgör, inte total fib-antal.**

A group can have 55 fibs and still be mostly map-OK if the fibs are spread across the
year. The map-unreadable signal is when many overlapping labels stack on a single swing
leg with no visual spacing. Examples:

- `2021` has 55 fibs but only the Jan–Mar 2021 zone is dense — the rest of the year is
  sparse and readable.
- `2023` has 17 fibs and is cleanly scannable — a map-OK baseline.
- `2017_h2` has 103 fibs across only 4 months — every leg is too crowded to read.

## Tier 2 Scope Decisions

### 2017_h2 — Full scope

All 103 fibs in the H2 2017 half-year (anchor_a year=2017, month ≥ 7). The Sep–Dec
2017 parabolic run stacked fibs at high density across the full group — no zone is
readable on the annual map. Per-fib zoom is needed for all.

### 2021 — Partial scope: Dec 2020 → Mar 2021 cluster

Only fibs with `anchor_a in [2021-01-01, 2021-04-01)` need per-fib zoom (~37 fibs).

**Selection logic:** The 2021 annual group groups by `anchor_a` year = 2021. Within
this group, the Jan–Mar 2021 zone is dense (BTC was in the initial parabolic bull leg
from the Nov 2020 lows). Dec 2020 fibs (anchor_a year=2020) are in the 2020 group,
which is map-OK — they do not contribute to the 2021 density problem. "Dec 2020 →
Mar 2021" describes the price action period; the anchor_a filter `< 2021-04-01` is
the clean implementation criterion.

Apr–Dec 2021 fibs are map-OK within the 2021 annual map — these do NOT need Tier 2.

## Log-Scale and X-Axis Notes

- **Y-axis log scale:** Confirmed — `ax.set_yscale("log")` is called at line 246 of
  `monthly_fib_map.py`. The y-axis is truly logarithmic. The visual appearance feels
  less dramatic than a 1M chart because the 4H maps span narrower price ranges.
- **X-axis month labels:** No explicit date formatter is set in `_draw_map`; mplfinance
  uses its own tick labeling. For Tier 1 maps (1 full year of 4H bars ≈ 2190 bars),
  the default tick density can make individual months hard to identify. The Tier 2 zoom
  charts (per-fib, much narrower window) will have significantly better x-axis
  readability.
